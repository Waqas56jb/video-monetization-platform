Mobile freeze — cause, fix, and a paired A/B on the same machine · 2026-09-17

WHAT WAS MEASURED
  scripts/measure-mobile-jank.mjs — Pixel 7 profile, CPU throttled 4x (a mid-range Android),
  Playwright Chromium. Per page: long tasks during load, then a scripted thumb-scroll watching
  the frame clock. "jank" = frames that arrived >100ms late; "LT#/LTms" = long tasks (>50ms)
  during the scroll and their total.

WHAT CAUSED IT (DevTools invalidation trace of one Home scroll, same profile)
  341 style recalculations and 340 layouts (192 forced) in ~80 scroll steps; 257 compositor commits.
  The trace's own reasons:
     538×  SPAN            — Animation    .vid-play span  (pulse, one per video card, forever)
     298×  .marquee-track  — Animation    the feature ticker (transform, forever)
     298×  ::after         — Animation
     164×  .skeleton  ×3   — Animation    the placeholder shimmer (background-position, forever)
      90×  InvalidateLayout from useScrolled (window.scrollY read in rAF) — 174ms forced layout
  CPU profile of the same scroll: JavaScript 6–9% of the window; "(program)" — Chromium's own
  style/layout/paint — 33–41%. Blocking playback warms or images changed nothing (checked).

THE FIX (client/src/styles/global.css touch block; client/src/hooks/useScrolled.js)
  On touch / low-power devices: .vid-play span, .marquee-track and .skeleton no longer animate
  (the ticker becomes a swipeable strip). useScrolled watches a 1px sentinel with an
  IntersectionObserver instead of reading scrollY on every scroll event.

PRODUCTION AFTER DEPLOY — count-based (scripts/trace-mobile-scroll.mjs; counts do not depend on machine load)
  after ca9a444:  style recalcs 1769 → 379 · layouts 305 → 171 · one animation still ticking every frame:
                  document.getAnimations() named `wheel · span.wheel::after` — the hero's mouse-wheel hint,
                  animating `top` (a layout property) forever in portrait.
  after f9158b1:  document.getAnimations() = 0 on load, at 1600px and mid-page ·
                  InvalidateLayout 344 → 43 (none from useScrolled) ·
                  the only "Animation" style recalcs left are the thumbnails' one-off fade-in (57×, composited opacity).
  Time-based numbers after the deploy were taken while this machine was busy (the user's Chrome on a
  call, three other dev servers) and swung 10× between identical runs; the paired A/B above is the
  reliable comparison.

WHAT IS LEFT, HONESTLY
  The paid watch page's worst gap (~1.2s during player boot at 4x throttle) is the Cloudflare
  Stream player's own iframe bundle (sdk-iframe-integration, 21% of the boot in the CPU profile)
  plus React's first render. It is not ours to shrink; deferring the iframe would move the
  pause, not remove it. The pre-roll/preview/entitlement flows depend on that boot and were not
  touched.
